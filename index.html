Notifications & Live Activities

Notifications show as a compact pill and expand into clean cards. Supports inline replies (drafts persist across interrupts), grouped app alerts, step image preview, and VoIP calls (WhatsApp, Telegram).

⛅

Rotating Idle Widgets

When idle, the cutout rotates through live widgets: clock with hourly ticks, Open-Meteo weather, net speed with daily usage, next calendar event, and alarms.

👆

Dual-Tier Gestures

Swipe left/right on the collapsed pill to skip tracks or cycle notifications without opening. On expanded cards, swipe up to dismiss, hold to clear all, or swipe down for freeform floating window.

🪪

Biometric Scan Overlays

Fluid biometric animations render directly over your camera cutout on face unlock, fingerprint unlock, and app lock prompts.

🎨

Material You & Customization

Follows your wallpaper color palette on Android 12+, with top-docked notch mode, punch-hole position calibration, and custom font support.

🎛️

30+ Events & Quick Launch

Over 30 system events (charging, audio jack, USB, VPN, downloads, backups) with independent toggles, plus a 2x4 utilities grid with resizable app widgets.

100% On-Device & Private

Zero Telemetry. 100% Private.

Smart Island processes all notification titles, message bodies, call states, and media information entirely on your device. There is no account creation, no analytics SDKs, and no cloud synchronization. Network access is used solely for optional weather widget lookups (via Open-Meteo) and user-initiated GitHub update checks.

🔒 Local Storage Only Notification history and preferences stay in local SQLite and DataStore on your device.
